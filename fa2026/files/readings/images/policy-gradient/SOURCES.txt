19-kl-tradeoff.svg
Original calculated figure for this handout; no externally sourced artwork or data.
Reproduce with make_kl_figure.py (NumPy and Matplotlib).
Model: R(A)=3, R(B)=1, q(A)=0.5, p(A)=sigmoid(theta), natural logarithms.
F_beta(p)=1+2p-beta*[p*log(2p)+(1-p)*log(2*(1-p))].
Beta values: 0, 1, 2. Curves sampled at 2,000 interior probabilities.
For beta>0, the marked optima are sigmoid(2/beta).
Typography and marks are vector paths for portable offline viewing.
